## Inspiration
As you might have guessed this work is inspired by Windows 11 wallpaper.

I wanted to reveal the concept’s soft yet lightweight structure.
The material clearly has some subsurface scattering, but achieving the final look came down mostly to compositing.

Here’s the simulation itself.
